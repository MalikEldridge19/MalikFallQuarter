Company - Coca-Cola

Project Template - CSR Trivia Challenge

Research of the 4 Pillars:

Economic - The Coca-Cola system contributes $85 billion annually to the U.S. Gross Domestic Product (GDP) and plans to invest $10 billion in the U.S. between 2024 and 2030.

Legal -  Coca-Cola never patented its beverage formula. Instead, it protects it under trade secret law for perpetual exclusivity, keeping the physical formula locked in an Atlanta vault rather than releasing it into the public domain after a 20-year patent term.

Ethical - The The Coca-Cola Company faces major ethical criticism across environmental, labor, tax, and human rights areas, despite official corporate codes of conduct and sustainability pledges.

Philanthropic - The Coca-Cola Company's philanthropy is primarily driven by The Coca-Cola Foundation, which has awarded over $1.8 billion in grants globally since it was established in 1984.
