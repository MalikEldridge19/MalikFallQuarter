Company - Coca-Cola

Project Template - CSR Trivia Challenge

Research of the 4 Pillars:

Economic: 
- The Coca-Cola system contributes $85 billion annually to the U.S. Gross Domestic Product (GDP) and plans to invest $10 billion in the U.S. between 2024 and 2030. 
- Coca-Cola's economic CSR mainly focus on sustainable profitability and community economic support.
- It also supports local economies by procuring ingredients from farmers under programs to improve yields and sustainability.

Legal:
- Coca-Cola never patented its beverage formula. Instead, it protects it under trade secret law for perpetual exclusivity, keeping the physical formula locked in an Atlanta vault rather than releasing it into the public domain after a 20-year patent term.
- Coca-Cola maintains extensive compliance policies, such as a global Code of Business Conduct, anti-bribery rules, a Responsible Marketing Policy, a Human Rights Policy, and Supplier Guiding Principles.
- Corporate filings do not break out CSR-specific metrics, but Coca-Cola discloses environmental data in sustainability updates. It pays statutory corporate taxes in each country.

Ethical: 
- The The Coca-Cola Company faces major ethical criticism across environmental, labor, tax, and human rights areas, despite official corporate codes of conduct and sustainability pledges.
- Coca-Cola explicitly addresses ethics through its Global Human Rights Policy, Supplier Guiding Principles, and responsible marketing codes.
- NGOs and ratings give mixed views. For example, Coke is consistently cited as a leader on recycling pledges but sometimes as a laggard in actual recycled content and packaging waste.

Philanthropic:
- The Coca-Cola Company's philanthropy is primarily driven by The Coca-Cola Foundation, which has awarded over $1.8 billion in grants globally since it was established in 1984.
- Coca-Cola channels philanthropy largely through The Coca-Cola Foundation and community initiatives. Major focus areas include clean water access, women’s empowerment, health and wellness, education, and disaster relief.
- The annual charitable giving from the company and the Foundation was $158.1M (2023), $174.9M (2024), and $162.5M (2025), which is about 1.5–1.7% of operating income.

Fun Facts:

- Coke sells 1.9 billion beverage servings per day worldwide. 
- In 2023 it replenished 148% of the water used in its drinks and aims for greater than 100% annually. 
- As of 2023, 90% of Coca‑Cola’s packaging was recyclable and 99% in 2025, but only around 14% of its volume is in reusable containers. 
- Coca‑Cola’s foundation has granted $1.8 billion to global causes over 40+ years, making around 200 grants yearly; in 2024 it gave $174.9M, which is around 1.6% of operating income. 
- Globally the Coca‑Cola system employs 700,000+ people.
